# Campus Marketplace landing: BRIEF

**Status:** partly interviewed. The user's request and four structural answers are
verbatim below. Everything marked *authored* was decided by the builder inside
that direction and is open to change.

## What the user asked for (verbatim)

> Redesign landing page
> Motion website
> More reactive and full pop icons and very interactive
> Choose colours according to material design rules
> The best good landing page
> Colours play an important roles

| Question | Answer |

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe** (*authored from the user's words*): pop, reactive, playful, colour-led, student. References: a sticker-covered laptop lid; Material 3 Expressive's springy shape morphs; a hostel room the night before move-out.
2. **Journey** (*authored*): what you need is already on campus → selling in group chats buries your post → the mess becomes a shop → real items, right now → how it works in three moves → your campus is open.
3. **Energy curve** (*authored*): bright open, a quiet dip, loudest at the tidy-up, steady through real items and steps, confident close.
4. **Feeling** (see curve below). The one moment: the tidy-up.
5. **What no other site does** (*authored*): your scroll physically tidies a messy hostel floor into a shop.
6. **Range** (user): pop and very interactive, so *playful*, nowhere near premium-minimal.
7. **Structure** (user): distinct scenes.
8. **Assets** (user): illustrated in code. Real listing photos and real listing counts come live from the database.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hero | Delight | Pop icons in Material shape containers float in three depth planes and dodge the pointer; the headline sits between planes |
| 2 Group chat | Recognition, a little friction | One quiet line assembles: the familiar way selling goes wrong. Calm ground, almost nothing moving (the silence before the peak) |
| 3 Tidy-up (PEAK) | Satisfaction, "oh!" | A messy floor of scattered items flies into labelled, colour-coded shelves as you scroll, each landing with a springy overshoot; live counts appear under each shelf |
| 4 Fresh drops | Appetite | Real newest listings slide sideways past you |
| 5 Three moves | Confidence | Post, chat, meet: a path draws itself through three pop icons |
| 6 Close | Resolve | Strong primary ground, one headline, the two actions; the page hands straight into the real marketplace grid |

**Peak:** "I scrolled and the whole messy room tidied itself into shelves, and each shelf showed how many things were actually for sale." Lives in act 3, gets the largest span.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where your scroll tidies a messy hostel floor into a shop, shelf by shelf.

**Authored silence:** act 2 is deliberately quiet (calm ground, one line, little motion) so the tidy-up has something to arrive from.

## Grammar (*authored, new*): Toybox

Distinct scenes, each a self-contained toy on its own Material colour ground, joined by hard cuts.

- **Nav:** a colour-chip rail. One chip per scene in that scene's Material role colour; the active chip morphs from circle to pill. Clicking jumps.
- **Sequence:** alternate toy (interactive) and read (one idea of type). Never two toys in a row without a read beat, except the peak.
- **Ending:** the toybox hands off into the real product: the close's primary action scrolls into the live listings grid that follows it.
- **Bans:** `scrub` (no video), `spotlight`, `magnet`, `drift` (grounds hard-cut per scene), full-frame scrims, invented numbers, centred copy in two consecutive scenes, more than one pinned act longer than 2 viewport-heights.

Why not the eight defined grammars: filmic one-shot and continuous world contradict "distinct scenes"; typographic poster has no room for pop icons; live surface bans the display type a landing needs; gallery/catalog forbids a hero claim; split stage collapses awkwardly on phones and caps interaction at one comparison; rhythmic cutlist bans `pin`, which the tidy-up needs to hold.

## Signature move

**The tidy-up.** Ten category objects lie scattered and rotated across a hostel floor. Scroll progress drives each one along its own path into a labelled shelf slot, with staggered timing and a spring overshoot, while its container morphs from a blob into its category shape and its Material custom colour fills in. Each shelf then shows its real live listing count, and clicking a shelf opens that category.

## Logged-in visitors (*authored*)

The full landing shows to signed-out visitors when no filter is active. Signed-in students get a short welcome strip instead, so the marketplace is one screen away.
